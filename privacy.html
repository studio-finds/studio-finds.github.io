Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 24, 2026

Studio Finds Publisher is an internal publishing tool used solely by the owner of the STUDIO FINDS Pinterest business account.

Information We Access

The application may access Pinterest account information, boards, Pins and related information belonging to the authenticated STUDIO FINDS account when required to manage and publish content.

How Information Is Used

Information accessed through Pinterest is used only for the internal operation of STUDIO FINDS, including identifying boards, creating Pins and managing published content.

Third-Party Users

Studio Finds Publisher is not offered to third-party users and does not connect to or manage other users' Pinterest accounts.

Sharing of Information

Pinterest data is not sold, rented or shared with third parties for advertising or unrelated commercial purposes.

Data Retention

Information is retained only for as long as necessary to operate and maintain the internal publishing workflow.

Changes to This Policy

This privacy policy may be updated when the functionality of Studio Finds Publisher changes.

Contact

Questions regarding this privacy policy may be directed to the operator of STUDIO FINDS.
